Color & accessibility checker

Accepts HEX, RGB, or HSL. Checks opaque sRGB color pairs against text contrast thresholds; this is not a complete accessibility audit. Click a palette swatch to copy its HEX value.

Image color picker

Click the image to sample an original source pixel. Keyboard: arrows move the source pixel; Enter samples it. Palette colors are approximate, extracted from downsampled bins; pixels below 50% opacity are ignored. Click a swatch to copy HEX. Limits: 20 MiB / 20 megapixels raster input.
